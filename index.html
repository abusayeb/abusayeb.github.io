eGP AutoPilot

2026
Founder · solo design, build and release

My first startup product: a bilingual (English/Bengali) desktop application that automates tender submission on the Bangladesh e-Government Procurement portal. It drives the portal through Playwright — login, form filling, encryption and signing, document mapping and a verification pass — and keeps going through crashes, network drops and half-finished runs.

⚡First startup product · v2.2.0, shipping on macOS and Windows
Case study

How it works

  • Drives the portal end to end with Playwright — login, Tenderer Info, Activities, Personnel and Priced BOQ forms, then click-sign, encrypt and save.
  • Resumes exactly where it stopped. Per-account state is written to disk, so a crash or a two-minute network drop does not cost the whole run.

  • Runs from a phone: a mobile dashboard over a REST API with QR-code pairing, and no credentials sent over the wire.
  • Firebase-backed accounts, subscriptions and bKash billing, with an admin panel for users, plans, devices and payments.
  • Packaged for macOS and Windows, behind 273 offline tests that never touch the production database.

Why I Saved This — browser memory

2026
Solo design, build and release

A browser extension that remembers why you saved something, not just where. It keeps the page, the passage you highlighted and your own note together, then brings you back to the exact spot months later with the note in the margin beside it.

⚡v1.3.0 · Chrome and Firefox
Case study

How it works

  • Save a page, a highlighted passage, a link or an image — from the toolbar, the context menu, or one shortcut that works out which you meant.
  • Search runs on your own notes and highlights first, ranking them above titles and URLs.
  • Re-anchors to the saved passage even when the page has changed since, scrolls to it, and lights up every highlight from that page with its note alongside.
  • Entirely local: no account, no server, no sync, no analytics, no network requests. Installing asks for no site access at all.
  • Licence keys are signed tokens verified on the device, because there is no server to check them against.
  • TypeScript and React on WXT, Dexie over IndexedDB, MiniSearch for retrieval, with unit tests in Vitest and end-to-end tests in Playwright.

RGEF — knowing when not to answer in skin-lesion screening

2026
First author · method, experiments and paper

A skin-lesion classifier that flags images it should not answer. RGEF fuses eight uncertainty signals — ensemble disagreement, evidential vacuity, conformal set size and five feature-space detectors — with a learned per-image reliability gate, accumulated as subjective-logic evidence.

⚡IEEE RAAICON 2026 (in press) · journal extension under review at BSPC
Case study

How it works

  • Gate conditioned on frozen self-supervised (DINOv2) features rather than the task backbone, giving each signal its own reliability per image.
  • The journal version filters the external archive against the training corpus by identifier and pixel hashing, removing 10,015 training-derived images before evaluation.
  • In the journal version, on the remaining 14,602 in-distribution and 666 out-of-distribution images, gated fusion reaches AUROC 0.7946 under acquisition shift, against 0.6945 for static fusion.
  • Reported honestly where it struggles: in the conference version, on smartphone images (PAD-UFES-20), the gain over simpler fusion is small.

Learning When to Look — adaptive perception for home robots

2026
First author · design, build and analysis

A preregistered closed-loop test of whether a navigating home robot can save detector compute by learning when to run it, where to look and which model size to use. Built in Habitat on scanned HM3D homes, with obstacles that reach the planner only through the detector.

⚡85% less detector compute · success criterion not met · under review at Intelligent Service Robotics
Case study

How it works

  • Controller learns when to run the detector, with hand-designed rules for where to look and which model size to use, a safety shield and an experience memory.
  • Navigation stack made to work in real-home scans: a static-map costmap layer, visibility-aware track ageing and floor-grounded obstacles took no-obstacle success from 0.55 to 0.95.
  • Task, validity gates and success criterion fixed in a preregistration; the comparison against twelve baselines was run once, with 120 paired episodes per policy in 25 held-out homes.
  • Reported numbers come only from a certification pipeline that checks code, data and provenance before a result can enter the paper.

HomeClean — doorways and robot vacuum cleaning

2026
First author · design, build and analysis

A parameter-free room-graph model that predicts how long a robot vacuum takes to clean a home from its floor plan, and where two robots obstruct each other. Tested in a simulation benchmark of 200 scanned homes with clutter, five cleaning strategies and two-robot teams.

⚡17,580 simulated runs · R² 0.81 on 100 unseen homes · under review at Robotics and Autonomous Systems
Case study

How it works

  • Turns a home's geometry into a Markov chain over rooms: exit rates from a billiard door law, per-point cleaning rates from Crofton's formula, no fitted parameters.
  • First preregistered study on 100 homes: the door law held (slope 0.86) and the model ranked homes well, but predicted cleaning faster than simulated.
  • Diagnosed the gap — clutter makes visits to a spot come in bursts — and fixed it with two corrections from the same billiard picture.
  • A second preregistration, frozen before 100 unseen homes were downloaded, confirmed the refined model, replicated the door law and showed a vacuum and a delivery robot jam at the doors delivery routes use most.

Forensic Invariant Learning for Synthetic Image Detection: Bridging Benford's Law and Topological Analysis with Machine Learning Ensembles

Md Abu Sayeb Rayhan, Ajoy Shutradhar, Hustagna HiyaFirst author

IEEE International Conference on Quantum Photonics, Artificial Intelligence and Networking (QPAIN) 2026

PublishedIEEE XploreScopus-indexed

Detecting AI-generated imagery through physics-informed invariants rather than learned artefacts. It combines Benford's Law digit statistics with topological features across a six-model ensemble, targeting cues that survive a change of generator. That is the failure mode most detectors share.

BD Eye-Doc: When Computer Vision Meets Conversational AI for Scalable Eye Disease Screening in Bangladesh

Ajoy Shutradhar, Md Abu Sayeb Rayhan, Mahmuda Khatun, Bishwajit DasSecond author

IEEE International Conference on Quantum Photonics, Artificial Intelligence and Networking (QPAIN) 2026

PublishedIEEE XploreScopus-indexed

An image-classification pipeline paired with a conversational interface, so screening can reach places where ophthalmologists are scarce. The hard question here is deployability in low-resource clinical settings rather than raw accuracy.

RGEF: Reliability-Gated Evidential Fusion for Robust Medical AI

Md Abu Sayeb Rayhan, Mosammat Fahimah, Md Tarek Mahmud, Hustagna Hiya, Ajoy Shutra DharFirst author

IEEE International Conference on Robotics, Automation, Artificial-Intelligence and Internet-of-Things (RAAICON) 2026

In pressIEEE

Out-of-distribution detection for skin-lesion diagnosis. A learned reliability gate weights each uncertainty signal per image and fuses them as evidence, so the model can flag inputs it should not answer instead of returning a confident wrong label.

Is CNN Dead? A Comprehensive Empirical Analysis of Convolutional Neural Networks, Ensemble Methods, and Vision Transformers for Rice Leaf Disease Classification in Low-Resource Agricultural Settings

Ajoy Shutra Dhar, Bishwajit Das, Md Mehedi Hassan, Md Abu Sayeb Rayhan, Halima Tuj Saydia, Shamima Akter ShimuFourth author

International Conference on Emerging Computing and Communication Technologies (ECCT) 2026 · Advances in Computer Science Research, Atlantis Press (Springer Nature)

In pressSpringer Nature

A controlled comparison of CNNs, ensembles and Vision Transformers under the compute and data constraints that agricultural deployment actually imposes. The strongest architecture on ImageNet is rarely the right choice there.

Does learning when to look pay for home service robots? A preregistered test of adaptive perception scheduling in closed-loop navigation

Md Abu Sayeb Rayhan, Hustagna Hiya, Ajoy Shutra DharFirst author

Intelligent Service Robotics (Springer)

Under reviewSpringer

Adaptive perception runs a robot's object detector only when and where it seems needed. Tested in a closed loop in 25 held-out scanned homes, against twelve baselines in a single preregistered run, the learned scheduler cut detector compute by 85% but reached the goal less often (55.0% vs 79.2%) and collided more, so it did not meet its success criterion. Running the cheapest adequate detector on every frame is a strong default.

Doorways set the pace of robot vacuum cleaning: a parameter-free room-graph model, tested and confirmed in 200 real homes

Md Abu Sayeb Rayhan, Hustagna Hiya, Ajoy Shutra DharFirst author

Robotics and Autonomous Systems (Elsevier)

Under reviewElsevier

A room-graph model with no fitted parameters turns a home's floor plan into a Markov chain over rooms, with exit rates set by the doorways, to predict how long a randomly moving robot vacuum takes to clean it and where two robots jam. Across 17,580 simulated runs in 200 scanned homes, a second preregistration frozen before 100 unseen homes were downloaded confirmed the refined model (R² 0.81) and the door law. Simulation only.

Input-conditional uncertainty fusion for skin lesion screening

Md Abu Sayeb Rayhan, Hustagna Hiya, Ajoy Shutra DharFirst author

Biomedical Signal Processing and Control (Elsevier)

Under reviewElsevier

Journal extension of RGEF. A gate conditioned on frozen self-supervised features gives each image its own reliability for eight uncertainty signals and fuses them as subjective-logic evidence, so a skin-lesion classifier can flag inputs it should not answer. On a deduplicated external archive it reaches AUROC 0.7946 under acquisition shift, against 0.6945 for static fusion.
